# Agency Brain & Systems

Skills for capturing the knowledge that normally disappears into meetings, chat, documents, and people's heads, then making that knowledge reusable across tools and models.

The durable asset is company knowledge and operating logic, not a specific LLM.

## Current skills

- `24 Meeting to Knowledge Capture` - extract durable decisions, rules, process changes, context, risks, and lessons with source provenance and human review
- `25 Agency Decision Logger` - record what was decided, why, what it affects, and when it should be revisited

## Operating principles

- keep changing business facts in authoritative systems or Agency Brain, not hardcoded inside skills
- preserve source, date, review state, and version history
- update or supersede knowledge instead of creating endless duplicates
- distinguish decisions, tasks, open questions, and durable knowledge
- keep the knowledge model usable by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Codex, MCP clients, and future models
