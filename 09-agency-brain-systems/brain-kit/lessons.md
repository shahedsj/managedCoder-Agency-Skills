# Lessons

What the agency learned the hard way. One entry per lesson. Keep each one short and honest.

A lesson is useful when someone facing a similar situation would act differently because of it.

## Good lessons

- A client project went over budget, and what we would do differently
- A hiring or delegation mistake, and the rule it created
- Something that worked and should be repeated

## Rules for this file

- Say what happened, then what you learned. Do not write a story.
- Give it a date and a source, even if the source is "my own memory".
- If a lesson became a rule, link it to the entry in `policies.md` and say so.
- Do not name people in a way that would embarrass them. Use roles.
