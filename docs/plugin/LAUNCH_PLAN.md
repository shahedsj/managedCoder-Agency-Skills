# Launch and marketing execution plan

Positioning: Practical agency operating workflows built from real owner experience. Start with your own notes. Learn the workflow before connecting systems.

## Offer
Free Agency Operations starter: six operating Skills plus Agency Context Pack setup. The plugin requires no signup. Keep education and external consulting content outside plugin operating instructions. Do not promise revenue gains, hours saved or live integrations without measured evidence.

## Four-week rollout
| Stage | Work | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Package, audit, synthetic workflow tests, privacy and release candidate | Reproducible archive and supported-host smoke test |
| Week 2 | Invite ten consenting agency owners to try their first client update and weekly review | Record results and fix critical errors |
| Week 3 | Submit directory package; prepare installation tutorial and three short workflow demos | Portal checks and human review |
| Week 4 | Publish verified listing link, demo and measured pilot findings on Shahed's channels and existing community | Approval, validated claims and usable install flow |

Before the real pilot passes, publish at most a clearly labeled preview. Do not change the existing website to claim a live plugin or tested results. Keep Learn → Build → Skill → Brain → Connect → Automate → Delegate → Operate as the education path. Starter ends at portable operating Skills and context. A future connected edition can reuse existing infrastructure after separate validation.
