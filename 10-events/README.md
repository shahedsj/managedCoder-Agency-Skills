# Events

Skills for planning, promoting, tracking and closing out agency events: webinars, live classes, in-person workshops and events co-hosted with partners or sponsors.

Events are where an agency meets many prospects at once. They also break quietly, through venues that were never booked, headcounts nobody can explain and follow-ups that never go out.
