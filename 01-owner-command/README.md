# Owner Command & Direction

Skills for deciding what deserves the owner's attention, what needs a decision, what should be delegated, and what should stop consuming founder time.

The goal is not to make the owner faster at doing everything. It is to help the owner see the business clearly enough to do fewer things personally.

## Current skills

- `06 Daily Task Triage` - work the highest-leverage owner tasks 3 at a time
- `10 Daily Owner Brief` - one-page daily operating picture
- `17 Meeting to Product Roadmap` - turn product conversations into roadmap signals
- `18 Weekly Owner Decision Brief` - separate DECIDE, UNBLOCK, DELEGATE, WATCH, and DROP
- `26 Next Meeting Prep Brief` - enter important meetings with history, open loops, questions, and one desired outcome
